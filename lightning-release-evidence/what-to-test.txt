This update fixes Lightning peer keepalive during chain scans, stops replaying an unfunded opening request after the provider rejects it, displays the provider's reason, and no longer counts requested channel capacity as wallet balance. Manual outbound funding starts with an empty amount. Rejected unfunded requests remain in history; funded channels are retained.

To test receiving: update the existing app without deleting wallet data, open Simple mode > Receive > Lightning, tap Sync and reconnect, then Get receiving capacity. Review the provider's setup quote and fee before approving. Do not use the Advanced manual channel request to buy inbound capacity. A setup fee buys capacity, not wallet balance. Pay an approved setup invoice externally, then sync and create a Lightning invoice once the channel is confirmed. Use that invoice for Kraken's Lightning withdrawal.

Mainnet uses real bitcoin. Standard Lightning receiving requires Winnow to stay open. This beta has no external watchtower; iOS background checks are opportunistic. The live provider rejection and reconnect were checked without authorizing or sending funds. Funded channel/payment/recovery interoperability is tested on regtest; funded mainnet receiving remains unverified.

On your physical device, test successful and canceled authentication, exact Copy/Share bytes, and locked-device storage behavior. These hardware checks remain pending. Report peer connection, sync, setup or receiving errors without sharing seeds, private keys or journals. Retain the existing app data while updating.

Funded channels warn when a verified check is missing, overdue or failed. Local reminders do not scan or broadcast justice transactions and their intervals are not safe offline limits.

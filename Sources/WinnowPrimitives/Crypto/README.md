# Hashing and encodings

Base58Check, Bech32 and Bech32m, PBKDF2, RIPEMD160 and BIP340 tagged hashes: the
byte-level rules behind addresses, keys and seeds. The compact-filter hashes
(GCS and SipHash) stay in WalletCore beside filter scanning; the
[topic guide](../../WalletCore/Crypto/README.md) covers both, and their tests.

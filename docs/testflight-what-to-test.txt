Winnow: one wallet view for multisig, Send from an account, drafts that survive, silent payment codes (internal test)

Send accepts a BIP352 silent payment code (sp1…/tsp1…). Advanced mode builds k-of-n Taproot multisig accounts from the signers under Settings → Signers: this phone, paired Ledgers, keys other people sent, or a whole account as one member. New in this build: the headline balance counts accounts, one Transactions list shows the wallet's and every account's payments, and an account is a source in Send. No MuSig2 or shared savings. Do not install on a phone holding funds in MuSig2 accounts (build 0.8.8) or vaults: it cannot see them.

One wallet view (Advanced and Simple)

1. With an account that holds coins: the Wallet balance is wallet plus accounts, with a line "of which … in N multisig accounts, needs co-signers". Simple mode shows the same line and the same list.
2. Transactions lists the account's receives and spends with the account's name under the title. A payment from the wallet to one of your accounts is one row, "Moved to <account>", never two.
3. A payment's screen shows the account; for one the phone broadcast that has not confirmed it offers Rebroadcast and Give up on this payment (frees the coins).

Send from an account, on signet

1. Two phones, Advanced mode, Settings → Signet; a 2-of-2 or 2-of-3 built under Wallet → Multisig → New multisig account (members from Settings → Signers), joined on the other phone with the descriptor, funded from a faucet.
2. First phone: Send → From: pick the account (the note names the policy). Paste an address, amount, Review payment: "Signatures: needs 2 of n". Sign and continue: authenticate, then the Collect signatures screen shows 1 of 2 and the PSBT to share.
3. Leave the screen. Wallet → Transactions shows "Awaiting signatures · <account>" on top with "1 of 2 signed"; tap it to resume. Trying a second payment from the account must be refused until this one is done or discarded.
4. Copy the PSBT to the second phone: Wallet → Multisig → the account → Payment awaiting signatures → paste it (Use this PSBT), Add this phone's signature, copy it back. Or Sign with Ledger, with a paired Ledger among the signers.
5. First phone: paste the signed copy (Combine): 2 of 2. Finalize and broadcast: the ordinary receipt. The row shows the account's name and confirms with the next block.
6. A silent payment code with an account picked must be refused. Discard draft frees the coins.

Backup mid-payment

With a draft or an unconfirmed account spend in place: Settings → Back up wallet (file) and the iCloud copy both succeed. Restore the file on a fresh install: the draft is back under Transactions; an unconfirmed spend shows with Rebroadcast.

Recovery path and nested accounts (unchanged)

Recovery after a delay still runs from the account's PSBT page: Recovery path, one signer alone spends a coin older than the delay; earlier it must refuse. A phone and its Ledger as one signer, and a 2-of-3 account as one member, build as before (Create approves the policy on the Ledger, app 2.2.1 or later); spends from them now also start under Send with the account picked.

Silent payment codes: in Send, paste a BIP352 code (tsp1… on signet) with Wallet as the source. The review names the code; the receipt shows the address paid. A mainnet code on signet must be refused.

Report bugs at https://github.com/winnowwallet/winnow/issues with app version, device, Ledger model, network and steps. Never include recovery words, private keys or backup files.

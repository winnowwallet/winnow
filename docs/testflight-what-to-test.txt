Winnow 0.8.2: Lightning in Winnow

New in 0.8.2: instant receive. With the new default provider, Megalith Instant, your first Lightning payment is one invoice: the provider opens your channel when the payment arrives and keeps a fee from it (at least 3,514 sats, so the first payment must be at least 23,514 sats). Winnow shows the fee before you approve. Until the channel confirms you trust the provider with that payment; the channel says "confirming" until then.

New in 0.8.1: the Lightning recovery file opens with your wallet's recovery phrase, so there is no second phrase to keep.

Lightning is now part of Winnow, a native Swift engine on Winnow's own wallet, verified chain scanner and broadcast path. Mainnet uses real bitcoin; start with small amounts.

Receiving

Simple mode: Receive, then Lightning (Bitcoin still gives an address). The default, Megalith Instant, opens the channel with your first payment: enter an amount, review the fee, approve, and pay the one invoice from another wallet. Olympus and Megalith (setup ahead of time) and LNServer (website setup) remain available. Enter the payment amount, which is separate from minimum channel capacity, and review the setup fee: approving shows an invoice without paying it. Pause an unpaid order and check it survives a restart.

Advanced mode has a Lightning tab. "Create reusable offer" makes an ordinary BOLT12 offer; its recipient must be online. "Create async receive offer" needs an async-capable provider; test with the recipient offline and after restarts. Only Settled confirms a payment.

Sending

Send → Pay Lightning invoice takes a BOLT11 invoice, an offer or a ₿name. Review the amount and maximum fee; cancelling the review must send nothing. Check settlement and history after a restart.

Channels

Fund a channel from your wallet, then close a small channel and check the funds return to your Bitcoin balance. On an anchor channel, review a recovery fee bump and its total cap: cancelling must leave your coins available. Deleting a wallet is refused while one of its channels is not yet closed.

Backups

iCloud backup still covers your Bitcoin wallet; it never holds channel state. Settings → Lightning recovery file exports an encrypted file that opens with this wallet's recovery phrase. Restore it on an empty device by entering that phrase, or with nothing typed after restoring the same wallet from iCloud. Never wipe your only funded wallet to test it. A file saved by 0.8.0 still opens with its separate phrase. A restored channel stays in recovery mode until it closes.

Background checks

Leave Background App Refresh on. Background checks also watch your channels and can relay their pre-signed recovery transactions; iOS decides when they run, never after force-quit. Check the channel-protection banner after a missed or failed check. There is no external watchtower.

Please check on a real device

Face ID and passcode approval (and cancelling it) for funding, paying and closing; the exact Share and Copy contents; and behaviour when locking and unlocking during a payment.

Upgrading

Your accounts, history, iCloud backup, routing and settings should be unchanged after the update.

Report bugs publicly at https://github.com/winnowwallet/winnow/issues. Include the app version, device, network, routing mode and steps to reproduce. Never include recovery words, private keys, wallet backup files or Lightning recovery files.

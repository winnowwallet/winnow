Winnow: multisig from a Signers list, a phone and its Ledger as one signer, silent payment codes (internal test)

Send accepts a BIP352 silent payment code (sp1…/tsp1…) and derives the recipient's address from the coins it spends. Advanced mode builds k-of-n Taproot multisig accounts from the signers under Settings → Signers: this phone, paired Ledgers, keys other people sent, or a whole account as one member. No MuSig2 or shared savings.

Do not install on a phone holding funds in MuSig2 accounts (build 0.8.8) or vaults: it cannot see them, and its iCloud backup leaves them out.

2-of-3 with two Winnows and a Ledger, on signet

1. On every phone: Advanced mode, Settings → Signet.
2. First phone: Settings → Signers → Pair a Ledger (Bitcoin Test open on it). It appears under Ledgers; nothing is approved on it yet.
3. Second phone: copy This phone's key from Settings → Signers and send it. First phone: Settings → Signers → Save keys someone sent.
4. First phone: Wallet → Multisig → New multisig account. Add a member → the Ledger; Add a member → the saved key. The preview must read "2 of 3: any 2 of the 3 keys sign a spend." Optionally Recovery after a delay (Fewer signers, 1 of 3, 1 day). Create account, approve it on the Ledger (app 2.2.1 or later): "Approved on <name>".
5. Paste the descriptor on the second phone under Join with a descriptor. Same first address on both; Verify on Ledger shows it.
6. Fund from a faucet; spend with any two (a phone's signature plus Sign with Ledger, or the PSBT pasted to the other phone and back); finalize and broadcast.
7. After the delay (144 blocks a day), New or pasted PSBT → Recovery path: one signer alone spends an old coin; before the delay it must refuse.

Also try: a separate recovery key; three phones without a Ledger (Add a member → Paste keys); declining on the Ledger (the account stays, its screen offers Register on Ledger); joining the same descriptor twice; renaming or removing a signer (built accounts are unchanged); a 2-of-2 account as one member of a new account (Add a member → Accounts on this phone; a 2-of-3 cannot be picked yet). None of this appears in Simple mode.

A phone and its Ledger as one signer (a nested account), on signet

1. Second phone: pair its Ledger, then Wallet → Multisig → Join with a descriptor: copy the two keys shown as "This phone and <Ledger>, as one signer" and send them. First phone: Save keys someone sent ("2 keys that always sign together: one signer").
2. First phone: New multisig account. On "This phone" tap the plus and pick the Ledger: "This phone + <Ledger>", "2 keys, one signer". Add a member → the saved pair, optionally a third. The preview must read "2 of 3 members: 3 ways to spend…". Create, approve on the Ledger. The account screen must say "2 of 3 members" and name each member's keys.
3. Second phone: Join with a descriptor says its Ledger holds a key of the account; Save and approve on that Ledger (or Register on Ledger from the account screen). Same first address on both.
4. Fund it. Create the PSBT on the first phone: one signature count per group, none complete. Sign with the phone and the Ledger: still none. On the second phone sign with both and paste it back: one group complete. Finalize and broadcast. A phone without its Ledger must never complete a group.

Silent payment codes

In Send, paste a BIP352 code (tsp1… on signet). The review names the code; the receipt shows the code and the address it paid, which the recipient's wallet finds. A mainnet code on signet must be refused. A code cannot be paid from a multisig account.

Backups

A backup carries multisig accounts and Ledger registrations; the iCloud copy also carries the signer list, merged on restore. A file with MuSig2 accounts or vaults must be refused, never restored partially.

Report bugs at https://github.com/winnowwallet/winnow/issues with app version, device, Ledger model, network and steps. Never include recovery words, private keys or backup files.

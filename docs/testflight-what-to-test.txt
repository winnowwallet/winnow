Device security check, round two (Settings, near the bottom)

The check now leads with a "Passcode set" row. If it says NO, that is the finding: without a passcode iOS never locks the wallet key, the locked check cannot mean anything, and the footer says so — set a passcode and come back.

With a passcode set: tap "Start locked-device check", lock the phone with the side button in the same motion, unlock after about twenty seconds. The probe now also samples at the moment the lock lands (not just on a fixed schedule), so even a quick suspension cannot lose the reading. "Locked read" should say "refused while locked — enforced"; the footnote lists each sample. Screenshot that screen, plus the app switcher showing Winnow's cover, plus the App uptime / Memory rows once the app has been resident a few days.

Vault coins now respect coinbase maturity

If a vault coin was mined directly to a vault address, its row in the vault detail says "matures in N blocks" and the Sign / combine review refuses to approve spending it early, with an error that counts the blocks remaining. Ordinary (non-coinbase) vault coins are unaffected.

Everything else

Normal use: receive, send, vault flows, backup. Under the hood every function in the wallet now passes a decision-path budget; nothing should feel different — if it does, that is worth a report.

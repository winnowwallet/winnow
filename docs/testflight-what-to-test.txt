Device security check (Settings, near the bottom)

This build turns your phone into the evidence the release gate still needs: proof that iOS refuses to hand Winnow the wallet key while the phone is locked.

Open Settings and find "Device security check". Confirm "Protection class" reads "when unlocked, this device only" and "Unlocked read" reads "allowed". Then tap "Start locked-device check" and immediately lock the phone with the side button. Unlock after about twenty seconds and come back: "Locked read" should say "refused while locked — enforced", and the footnote should list four samples with status −25308 marked "(locked)". Screenshot that screen. If it says the phone never locked, you tapped and locked too slowly — just run it again.

Also in that section: "App uptime" and "Memory". After the app has been resident for a day or more, screenshot those too — that is the long-soak reading.

App switcher

With the wallet on screen, swipe up into the app switcher and look at Winnow's card: it must show the privacy cover, not your balance or addresses. A screenshot of the switcher is evidence.

Everything else

Normal use: receive, send, vault flows, backup. Nothing else changed in this build; if anything feels different, that is worth a report.

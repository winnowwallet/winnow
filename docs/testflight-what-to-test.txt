Winnow on Swift-only curve math, with Taproot multisig and a Ledger signer (internal test)

Every address and signature comes from Winnow's own Swift secp256k1 module instead of libsecp256k1. The module is variable-time and does not clear secrets from memory; that is accepted for this test build. Advanced mode adds raw k-of-n Taproot multisig accounts, and a Ledger can be the second signer of a 2-of-2 over Bluetooth. There is no MuSig2, no 2-of-3 vault and no shared savings. Compact-filter sync, peer routing, people, labels and backups are unchanged.

Do not install this build on a phone that holds funds in MuSig2 accounts (build 0.8.8) or shared-account vaults. It cannot see them, and its automatic iCloud backup leaves them out. Use a phone with only an ordinary wallet, or a fresh install.

Ledger 2-of-2 on signet

1. On the Ledger (Bitcoin app 2.1 or later), install and open Bitcoin Test.
2. In Winnow, turn on Advanced mode and switch to signet (Advanced → Settings → Signet).
3. Wallet tab → Multisig → Pair a Ledger (2-of-2). Name the account, tap Pair, and confirm the same pairing code on the phone and the Ledger the first time.
4. On the Ledger, check the account and both keys, then approve.
5. Open the account and tap Verify on Ledger: the address on the Ledger must match the phone's.
6. Fund that address from a signet faucet and wait for it to confirm.
7. New or pasted PSBT: enter a signet address and amount, Create PSBT, Add this phone's signature, then Sign with Ledger and check the payment on its screen before approving. Try the other order too.
8. Finalize and broadcast, then watch the payment and its change confirm.

Also try: declining on the Ledger (nothing is signed), Bluetooth off, the Ledger locked or on its dashboard, and pairing the same Ledger again (the same account comes back, not a copy). Ledger signing is not offered in Simple mode.

Ordinary wallet

Receive from a faucet, send a payment and watch it confirm. Restore the same recovery words in the App Store Winnow on another device: the first receive address must match. Pay a saved person and a pasted address. Bump the fee on a pending payment.

Backups

A backup from this build carries its multisig accounts and Ledger pairings. A file carrying MuSig2 accounts or vaults must be refused with a clear message, never restored partially.

Report bugs publicly at https://github.com/winnowwallet/winnow/issues. Include the app version, device, Ledger model and app version, network and steps to reproduce. Never include recovery words, private keys, or wallet backup files.

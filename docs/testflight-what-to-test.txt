Block explorer picker (Settings)

The warned external explorer is now a picker: blockstream.info (the new default), mempool.space, or Custom with an Esplora-compatible URL field. Your selection persists per network, and if you had typed a custom URL before, it stays selected as Custom. One deliberate wrinkle: blockstream.info has no signet explorer, so while on signet that preset opens mempool.space — the "Selected:" line shows exactly where a tap will go. Try each preset, tap a transaction from history, and confirm the warning names the right site.

Under the hood

The Bitcoin implementation now lives in its own repository (btc-swift) and the app pins an exact revision of it; every function in both is at seven decision paths or under, enforced by CI. None of that should feel like anything — receive, send, vault flows, and backup should behave exactly as before, and anything that doesn't is worth a report.

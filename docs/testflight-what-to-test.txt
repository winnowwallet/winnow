Winnow: multisig from a Signers list, a 2-of-3 account or a phone with its Ledger as one signer, silent payment codes (internal test)

Send accepts a BIP352 silent payment code (sp1…/tsp1…) and derives the recipient's address from the coins it spends. Advanced mode builds k-of-n Taproot multisig accounts from the signers under Settings → Signers: this phone, paired Ledgers, keys other people sent, or a whole account as one member, signing as its own policy says. No MuSig2 or shared savings. Do not install on a phone holding funds in MuSig2 accounts (build 0.8.8) or vaults: it cannot see them.

2-of-3 with two Winnows and a Ledger, on signet

1. On every phone: Advanced mode, Settings → Signet.
2. First phone: Settings → Signers → Pair a Ledger (Bitcoin Test open). Nothing is approved on it yet.
3. Second phone: copy This phone's key from Settings → Signers and send it. First phone: Save keys someone sent.
4. First phone: Wallet → Multisig → New multisig account. Add a member → the Ledger, then the saved key. Preview: "2 of 3: any 2 of the 3 keys sign a spend." Optionally Recovery after a delay (Fewer signers, 1 of 3, 1 day). Create, approve on the Ledger (app 2.2.1 or later).
5. Second phone: Join with a descriptor. Same first address on both; Verify on Ledger shows it.
6. Fund from a faucet; spend with any two (a phone plus Sign with Ledger, or the PSBT pasted to the other phone and back); finalize and broadcast.
7. After the delay (144 blocks a day), New or pasted PSBT → Recovery path: one signer alone spends an old coin; earlier it must refuse.

Also try: a separate recovery key; three phones without a Ledger (Paste keys); declining on the Ledger (the account stays; Register on Ledger is offered); joining the same descriptor twice; renaming or removing a signer. None of this appears in Simple mode.

A phone and its Ledger as one signer (a nested account)

1. Second phone: pair its Ledger; under Join with a descriptor copy the two keys shown as one signer and send them. First phone: Save keys someone sent ("2 keys … one signer").
2. First phone: New multisig account. On "This phone" tap the plus and pick the Ledger: "This phone + <Ledger>". Add a member → the saved pair, and a third. Preview: "2 of 3 members: 3 ways to spend…". Create, approve on the Ledger.
3. Second phone: Join with a descriptor says its Ledger holds a key; Save and approve on it. Same first address on both.
4. Fund it. Create the PSBT: one count per group, none complete. Sign with the phone and the Ledger: still none. The second phone signs with both and pastes it back: one group complete. Finalize and broadcast.

A 2-of-3 account as one member

1. With the 2-of-3 above on this phone: New multisig account → Add a member → Accounts on this phone → the 2-of-3 ("any 2 of its 3 keys"; This phone moves into it), then two saved keys. Preview: "2 of 3 members, one of them 2 of 3: 7 ways to spend…". Recovery after a delay → Fewer signers, any 1 of 3 members. Create. The account screen shows the member as "any 2 of:" its keys and Recovery "any 1 of the members". A nested account cannot be picked.
2. Fund it. A spend finishes only with two of that account's keys plus a whole other member; after the delay one member alone recovers.
3. With the paired Ledger inside the 2-of-3, Create approves the seven-leaf policy on it; a larger tree is refused (a Ledger takes 512 characters).

Silent payment codes: in Send, paste a BIP352 code (tsp1… on signet). The review names the code; the receipt shows it and the address paid. A mainnet code on signet must be refused. Not from a multisig account.

Backups, file and iCloud, carry multisig accounts, Ledger registrations and the Signers list; the list merges with the phone's on restore. A file with MuSig2 accounts or vaults must be refused.

Report bugs at https://github.com/winnowwallet/winnow/issues with app version, device, Ledger model, network and steps. Never include recovery words, private keys or backup files.

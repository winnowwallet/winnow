Winnow on Swift-only curve math: 2-of-3 multisig with recovery paths and raw MuSig2 accounts (internal test)

Every address and signature comes from Winnow's own Swift secp256k1 module instead of libsecp256k1. The module is variable-time and does not clear secrets from memory; that is accepted for this test build. Advanced mode builds k-of-n Taproot multisig accounts from Winnows or any signer, with an optional recovery path, and holds raw n-of-n MuSig2 accounts. There are no shared savings or cosigner cards. Compact-filter sync, peer routing, people, labels and backups are unchanged.

Do not install this build on a phone that holds funds in shared-account vaults. It cannot see them, and its automatic iCloud backup leaves them out. MuSig2 accounts from build 0.8.8 carry over.

2-of-3 with three Winnows, on signet

1. On every phone, turn on Advanced mode and switch to signet (Advanced → Settings → Signet).
2. On the second and third phones: Wallet → Multisig → Join with a descriptor, and copy This phone's key. Send both to the first phone.
3. On the first phone: Wallet → Multisig → New multisig account. Name it, paste both keys, keep "Any 2 of 3", and optionally turn on Recovery after a delay (try Fewer signers, 1 of 3, after 1 day). Tap Create account.
4. Copy the descriptor it shows and paste it on the other two phones under Join with a descriptor. All three phones must show the same first address.
5. Fund the address from a signet faucet and wait for it to confirm.
6. Spend with any two: create the PSBT on one phone, add its signature, paste the PSBT on another phone and sign there, and paste it back. Finalize and broadcast.
7. After the delay (144 blocks a day), New or pasted PSBT → Recovery path: one signer alone can spend a coin that has not moved. Before the delay it must refuse.

Also try: a separate recovery key instead of fewer signers, a 2-of-2 of two phones, and joining the same descriptor twice (the same account comes back, not a copy). None of this appears in Simple mode.

MuSig2 accounts

On signet, in Advanced mode, copy this phone's key from Wallet → Add MuSig2 account, build tr(musig(PHONE_KEY,OTHER_KEY)/<0;1>/*) with a second signer such as Bitcoin Core, paste it and check the first address on both. Fund it, create a PSBT, add this phone's nonce, combine the other signer's, add the partial signature, combine theirs and broadcast. Leaving the screen between rounds must discard the exchange.

Ordinary wallet

Receive from a faucet, send a payment and watch it confirm. Restore the same recovery words in the App Store Winnow on another device: the first receive address must match. Pay a saved person and a pasted address. Bump the fee on a pending payment.

Backups

A backup from this build carries its multisig accounts and MuSig2 accounts. A file carrying vaults must be refused with a clear message, never restored partially.

Report bugs publicly at https://github.com/winnowwallet/winnow/issues. Include the app version, device, network and steps to reproduce. Never include recovery words, private keys, or wallet backup files.

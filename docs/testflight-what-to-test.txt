Winnow on Swift-only curve math: 2-of-3 multisig with recovery paths, and a Ledger signer (internal test)

Every address and signature comes from Winnow's own Swift secp256k1 module instead of libsecp256k1. The module is variable-time and does not clear secrets from memory; that is accepted for this test build. Advanced mode builds k-of-n Taproot multisig accounts from Winnows, a Ledger or any signer, with an optional recovery path. There is no MuSig2 and no shared savings. Compact-filter sync, peer routing, people, labels and backups are unchanged.

Do not install this build on a phone that holds funds in MuSig2 accounts (build 0.8.8) or shared-account vaults. It cannot see them, and its automatic iCloud backup leaves them out.

2-of-3 with two Winnows and a Ledger, on signet

1. On every phone, turn on Advanced mode and switch to signet (Advanced → Settings → Signet).
2. On the second phone: Wallet → Multisig → Join with a descriptor, and copy This phone's key. Send it to the first phone.
3. On the first phone: Wallet → Multisig → New multisig account. Name it, paste the second phone's key, turn on Include a Ledger, keep "Any 2 of 3", and optionally turn on Recovery after a delay (try Fewer signers, 1 of 3, after 1 day). Open Bitcoin Test on the Ledger (version 2.2.1 or later for a recovery path) and tap Create account; approve the account on the Ledger.
4. Copy the descriptor it shows and paste it on the second phone under Join with a descriptor. Both phones must show the same first address; Verify on Ledger shows it on the Ledger too.
5. Fund the address from a signet faucet and wait for it to confirm.
6. Spend with any two: create the PSBT on one phone, add its signature, then Sign with Ledger, or paste the PSBT on the other phone and sign there, and paste it back. Finalize and broadcast.
7. After the delay (144 blocks a day), New or pasted PSBT → Recovery path: one signer alone can spend a coin that has not moved. Before the delay it must refuse.

Also try: a separate recovery key instead of fewer signers, a 2-of-3 of three phones without a Ledger, declining on the Ledger, and joining the same descriptor twice (the same account comes back, not a copy). None of this appears in Simple mode.

Ordinary wallet

Receive from a faucet, send a payment and watch it confirm. Restore the same recovery words in the App Store Winnow on another device: the first receive address must match. Pay a saved person and a pasted address. Bump the fee on a pending payment.

Backups

A backup from this build carries its multisig accounts and Ledger pairings. A file carrying MuSig2 accounts or vaults must be refused with a clear message, never restored partially.

Report bugs publicly at https://github.com/winnowwallet/winnow/issues. Include the app version, device, Ledger model and app version, network and steps to reproduce. Never include recovery words, private keys, or wallet backup files.

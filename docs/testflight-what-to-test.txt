Winnow 0.7.11: private by default through your own Tor and I2P gateways

Winnow does not include Tor or I2P. With Tailscale connected and gateways named winnow-tor-gateway (port 9050) and winnow-i2p-gateway (port 4447) on your tailnet, Automatic routing now connects only through them, so no Bitcoin peer sees your IP address. Setup: https://github.com/winnowwallet/winnow/blob/main/docs/peer-gateways.md

Home screen banner

On mainnet, simple mode shows how peers are reached. With Tailscale connected it should turn green: "Private: peers reached through Tor and I2P" (or just Tor or I2P). With Tailscale off, reopen Winnow: it should turn orange and say peers can see your IP address. Turn Tailscale back on and return to Winnow: it should go green again.

Automatic without clearnet

In Advanced → Settings → Connected peers, tap Refresh. With both gateways active, every peer should be labelled Tor or I2P, never Clearnet, and the three peers should not all be on one network. Sync, Receive and Send should still work; the first connection can take 10 to 30 seconds.

Manual routing

Choose Manual, enable only Tor peers, enter the Tor gateway, and tap Apply routing: every peer should be labelled Tor. Repeat with only I2P peers: tap Refresh peer list (it downloads from the I2P mirror), then check that every peer is labelled I2P. Turn Tailscale off in a Tor-only or I2P-only mode: the wallet should stop connecting rather than use clearnet. Choose Automatic again when done.

Backup

Check whether the home screen says Backed up for iCloud, and that the delete-wallet wording reads clearly.

Upgrading from an older version

Check that your accounts, transaction history, backups and saved settings remain available after the update.

Report bugs publicly at https://github.com/winnowwallet/winnow/issues. Include the app version, device, network, routing mode and steps to reproduce. Never include recovery words, private keys, or wallet backup files.

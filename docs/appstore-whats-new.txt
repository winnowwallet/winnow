• Private by default with your own gateways. If you run Tor or I2P on your Tailscale network, Winnow finds the gateways automatically and then connects only through them, so no Bitcoin peer sees your IP address. Winnow itself includes no Tor or I2P.
• A banner on the home screen shows whether peers are reached privately through Tor or I2P, or directly and can see your IP address.
• Advanced → Peer networks labels each connected peer as Tor, I2P or clearnet, and lets you require Tor, I2P or both. An unavailable gateway never falls back to a direct connection there.
• With I2P alone, the signed peer list downloads from its I2P mirror, so no public website is contacted.

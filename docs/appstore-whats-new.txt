• Beginner mode is one screen: your balance and whether it is up to date, Receive and Send, your activity in plain words, shared savings once you have any, and the backup. No tabs, no settings, nothing technical.
• Advanced mode is the full three-tab interface, one tap from the corner of that screen, with fee controls, network settings, signing tools and every setting. Simple brings the one screen back and keeps what you set.
• Shared savings start from one place: "Save with someone…", to start savings with people you have added or join savings someone shared with you.
• Backing up the wallet is a row on the screen, not a setting.

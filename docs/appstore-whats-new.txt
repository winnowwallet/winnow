• Private by default with your own gateways. If you run Tor or I2P on your Tailscale network, Winnow finds the gateways and then connects only through them, so no Bitcoin peer sees your IP address. Winnow itself includes no Tor or I2P.
• A banner on the home screen shows whether peers are reached privately through Tor or I2P, or directly and can see your IP address.
• Background checks. When iOS allows, Winnow checks the chain for new payments while it is closed, through the same peers and routing it uses when open.
• Automatic iCloud backup. Your wallet is saved, encrypted, to your private iCloud and restores with simple onboarding. You can turn it off in Advanced.
• Your wallet key sits behind the Keychain's own Face ID or passcode check.
• A simpler home screen. Simple mode is one screen; everything else is in Advanced.
• Peers come from a signed daily census instead of a bundled list, and every verified Tor and I2P peer is kept.
• Simple mode no longer shows an error each time a peer drops mid-sync.

• Every key that can approve a shared-savings payment is now listed with a fingerprint — the person it belongs to, you, or a key that is not one of your people — and your own card shows the same fingerprint for co-owners to compare.
• Your saved recipients and shared accounts are now sealed under a key held in the device's protected storage, so a file that Winnow did not write on this phone is refused rather than trusted.
• The recovery phrase hides itself while the screen is being recorded or mirrored.
• Peer discovery is harder to steer: automatic peers of one kind can no longer outvote the others on which filters are genuine, headers dated far in the future are refused, and a peer that promises headers and delivers none is dropped.
• A custom block explorer must use https. Peer connections use less memory over long sessions and drop peers that stop answering.

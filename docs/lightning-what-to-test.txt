Winnow Lightning, a research beta. Mainnet uses real bitcoin; signet and regtest use test coins.

First build from the main Winnow repository: an existing Winnow Lightning wallet and its channels should open unchanged.

This version uses Winnow's Swift wallet, transactions and verified chain scanner with a native Swift Lightning engine. It uses a separate Swift storage namespace and preserves older Winnow Lightning wallet files. Background checks share the wallet’s verified chain scanner and can relay pre-signed channel recovery transactions while locked after the first unlock. iOS chooses when to run them; they cannot run after force-quit or while the phone is off. There is no external watchtower.

A fresh install should discover public mainnet Bitcoin peers automatically. Switch networks in Settings and check that each wallet and Lightning node identity survives switching and relaunching. Existing regtest installations retain their wallet and can switch to mainnet or public signet; only regtest needs a private Bitcoin test node.

Simple mode: Receive, then Lightning. Mainnet providers are Olympus and Megalith (in-app setup), or LNServer (website setup). Enter the intended payment amount, distinct from minimum channel capacity. Review the setup fee: approval exposes an invoice without paying it. Pause an unpaid order and check it survives restart. BOLT11 receiving needs confirmed capacity; keep Winnow open.

Advanced mode: Lightning, then Create reusable offer in the Receive section is ordinary BOLT12. Its recipient must be online to issue the invoice and receive payment; no async provider is required. Create async receive offer is separate and needs an async-capable provider and configured path. For async, test an offline recipient and client restarts with one reconciled history record. Awaiting recipient is pending; only Settled confirms payment.

Send a BOLT11 invoice, ordinary offer or BIP353 name. Review amount and maximum fee; canceled review must submit nothing. Verify settlement and history after restart. On an anchor channel, review recovery fee bump and total cap: cancellation must leave coins available; after authorization, an interrupted fee bump resumes its saved signed transaction and cap. Existing static-remotekey channels are preserved. Close a disposable channel and check returned funds. Regtest interoperability does not establish funded mainnet behavior.

Physical-device checks are pending for this beta: test successful and canceled Face ID/passcode approval for funding, payment and close; cancellation must not submit an action. Check exact Share/Copy contents and report behavior when locking and unlocking the device. Leave Background App Refresh enabled and check the last complete chain-check time after returning to the app. Check that wallet sync resumes, channel identities remain stable, and no spending authorization appears during background checks. Simulator runs do not establish locked-device journal or Keychain protection.

Check channel-protection banners and permitted reminders after incomplete or stale chain checks. Only a completed verified scan updates the last-check time. Warnings do not protect funds or guarantee iOS background checks.

Export an encrypted recovery file and keep its separate 24-word wrapping phrase safely. Restore only into a separate empty destination; never wipe the only funded wallet to test. Restored Lightning is permanently recovery-only: scan returned funds and contact original peers, without resuming payments or publishing old commitments. A peer may delay recovery until it closes. Keep the original device and files while channels or recovery are outstanding. This research wallet has no cloud backup or wallet deletion.

The channel journal is schema 5; older betas refuse it, so do not downgrade while channels or recovery remain outstanding. Background checks cover the selected network only.
